☕ Dirty Matcha

A harmony of

EXPRESSO & MATCHA

Matcha Coffee is a modern fusion drink that brings together the earthy, refreshing flavor of Japanese matcha with the rich and bold taste of coffee. Matcha itself originated in China and was later developed and deeply associated with Japanese tea culture, while espresso was developed in Italy.To make it, high-quality matcha powder is first whisked with a small amount of warm water until smooth and frothy. Milk is then added, followed by ice for a refreshing iced version. Finally, a shot of freshly brewed espresso is poured over the matcha and milk, creating beautiful green and brown layers. The result is a creamy, slightly sweet, earthy, and pleasantly bitter drink that combines the calming character of matcha with the strong energy of espresso. It is especially popular among people who enjoy both tea and coffee and want to experience something unique and visually attractive.
